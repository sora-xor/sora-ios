## Confirmed defects

- iOS and Android retained mnemonic policies admitted 12, 15, and 24 words but rejected valid 18- and 21-word phrases accepted by released importers. Those accounts need SORA2-only legacy classification; Nexus derivation remains limited to the supported 12/24-word sources.
- iOS version-one mapping deduplicated by decoded public key, silently skipping distinct retained addresses on different networks. The inventory check subsequently rejected the migration. Every distinct source account must survive the mapping.
- Android releases before the multi-account model stored the registered account in encrypted preferences. Current startup rejected those intact credentials when the database lacked account rows. Old Room versions also lacked an additive migration path.
- iOS 1.x stored both `privateKey` and `seedEntropy`. The new Root policy rejected the pair even when both records described the same wallet. The released Iroha derivation can authenticate that relationship without deleting either record.
- Android's deferred recovery startup scheduled Nexus and then SORA2 in one coroutine without containing scheduling errors. A WorkManager initialization/enqueue exception could crash startup and prevent the other network's recovery from being scheduled.
